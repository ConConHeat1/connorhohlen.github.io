Community Rules

Rules for Orbit Shift leaderboards and friends. Effective September 14, 2026.

Choose a respectful username

Use 3–16 letters, numbers, or underscores. Names are unique without regard to capitalization. Do not use hateful, sexual, threatening, harassing, impersonating, or personally identifying names. Filtering can make mistakes; contact support if an appropriate name is rejected.

Play fairly and respect others

Do not forge scores, automate abuse, bypass security, spam friend requests, or evade blocks. A shared friend code is an invitation to request friendship, not automatic acceptance. Do not share another person's private information.

Report, block, and moderation

Use the menu next to a player to report an inappropriate username or block the player. Blocking removes the friendship and prevents further requests between those profiles. Reports are reviewed by the developer. We may replace inappropriate usernames, reject or correct invalid scores, or suspend access to online features. Email connorhohlen@gmail.com about a report or to request review of a moderation decision.

Your profile and purchases

Online services require connectivity and may be temporarily unavailable. Anonymous profiles are tied to the installation and do not provide guaranteed recovery across devices. Deleting a profile is permanent. Local play and store purchases are separate; Apple and Google purchases do not transfer between platforms. See the privacy policy and deletion options.
