Contact

Get help directly.

Email support

Email connorhohlen@gmail.com with the app version, device model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened. Do not include passwords, payment-card details, or other sensitive information.

Quick help

Common questions.

Restore Remove Ads

Open the in-game settings and choose Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account that originally purchased Remove Ads. Apple handles the transaction and restoration.

Rewarded ad did not grant a reward

A reward is granted only after the advertising provider confirms completion. If an ad is closed early or the network fails, gameplay should return safely without granting the reward.

Game Center is unavailable

Confirm that Game Center is enabled in iOS Settings and that the device is online. Scores earned offline may be submitted later when the service becomes available.

Reset local data

Orbit Shift stores game progress and preferences on the device. Removing the app and its data removes that local information. A previously purchased Remove Ads entitlement can be restored through Apple.
