Custom FPV Drone

A quadcopter I built from individual components to learn electronics, soldering, flight-controller configuration, radio communication, and system troubleshooting.

  • Electronics
  • Soldering
  • Betaflight
  • SBUS
  • FPV

Why I built it

In high school, I wanted to build my own FPV drone and understand what made it fly. I picked the parts, soldered the connections, and used Betaflight Configurator to get the hardware working together. It was a hands-on way to learn how small wiring and configuration details affect a whole system.
