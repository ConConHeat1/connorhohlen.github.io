Flexin

Flexin is a social fitness app I’m developing around workout Check-Ins, training progress, and connecting with friends.

  • Flutter
  • Dart
  • Firebase
  • Firestore
  • iOS

In development

What it is

Flexin gives each workout a place in a friend feed through a photo or short video Check-In. Profiles keep selected milestones, while training tools help organize progress.
